Research Messaging Platform

EMA Research Messaging

A secure platform used by research teams to schedule ecological momentary assessment (EMA) prompts and deliver participant survey links by text message.

Login

What This Project Does

Participant Coordination

Research staff can enroll participants, define study windows, and manage scheduling settings from one admin interface.

Timed Survey Invitations

The system sends recurring SMS invitations for EMA windows and daily follow-up surveys based on configured schedules.

Compliance Visibility

Study staff can review delivery activity and participation data to support protocol adherence and operational quality.

Messaging Program Notice

Participants who opt in may receive recurring research-related text messages, including survey prompts and reminders. Message frequency varies by study configuration. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe.
