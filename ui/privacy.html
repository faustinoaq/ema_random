Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to operate research messaging workflows, including:

  • Mobile phone number and participant identifier provided for study enrollment.
  • Messaging opt-in and opt-out status, including STOP and HELP interactions.
  • Message delivery metadata such as timestamps, status, and survey-link activity records.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Send recurring research-related SMS messages, reminders, and survey invitations.
  • Manage study schedules, monitor delivery health, and support research operations.
  • Maintain system security, prevent abuse, and document compliance activity.

Mobile Information and Consent Non-Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or otherwise provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent information to any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Data sharing is limited to service providers that help us deliver messaging and platform operations, and only for those operational purposes.

Messaging Disclosures

By opting in, you agree to receive recurring text messages related to your enrolled research program. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for help.

Data Retention and Security

We retain information only as needed for research administration, auditability, and legal obligations. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect stored data.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the research support team through the official study channel listed in your enrollment materials.
